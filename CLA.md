This agreement covers contributions to the Rudder project (this repository).
It exists so that the project can (a) distribute contributions under its
license and (b) later adopt **additional permissions under section 7 of the
GNU AGPL version 3** (for example, a plugin exception) without contacting
every past contributor — and for **no broader purpose**. In particular, it is
deliberately **not** a general relicensing or copyright-assignment agreement.

**Definitions.**
- "Tyche" — Tyche Technologies Co., Ltd.
- "Project" — the Rudder software in this repository, licensed under
  AGPL-3.0-only.
- "Contribution" — any original work of authorship you intentionally submit
  to the Project.
- "AGPL" — the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only
  (SPDX: `AGPL-3.0-only`).

## Part B — Corporate Contributor License Agreement (Draft)

For a corporation (or other legal entity) whose employees or contractors
contribute to the Project on its behalf.

### B1. Grants

The corporation makes the same grants and representations as in Part A
(sections A1–A5, applied to the corporation as the rights holder), for
Contributions submitted by its designated employees or contractors. The same
limits apply: the corporation retains its copyright; the license-stewardship
grant in A3 is limited to AGPL §7 **additional permissions** while the
Project remains AGPL-3.0-only, and includes no proprietary-relicensing right.

### B2. Designated contributors

The corporation identifies the employees or contractors authorized to submit
Contributions on its behalf in a **designated-contributor schedule** (a
list of names and GitHub accounts) submitted when this agreement is
executed. The schedule is maintained as follows:

1. An **authorized representative** of the corporation notifies Tyche of
   additions or removals through the CLA contact channel (§A8). A change
   takes effect when Tyche records it; Tyche's recorded copy of the
   schedule is authoritative.
2. A Contribution submitted by a person **not on the schedule** at the time
   of submission is not covered by this Corporate CLA and instead requires
   an Individual CLA (Part A).
3. Removal from the schedule is prospective only: it does not affect
   licenses already granted for Contributions submitted while the person
   was listed.

### B3. Authority

The person executing this agreement represents that they are authorized to
bind the corporation.

### B4. Administrative terms

Same as §A8: governing law (Republic of Korea), jurisdiction (Seoul Central
District Court, exclusive, first instance), contact (the Project's GitHub
issue tracker), and signing mechanism (CLA-check bot flow, executed for the
corporation by an authorized representative) apply equally to this Part B.
